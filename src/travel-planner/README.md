# Odyssey

Odyssey is an AI travel planning assistant. Tell it your destination, dates, and budget — it handles the rest: finding flights, recommending hotels, building a day-by-day itinerary, and estimating total costs.

## What it does

- **Flight search** — finds and ranks flight options for your route and dates
- **Hotel recommendations** — suggests stays matched to your budget and preferences
- **Itinerary planning** — builds a structured day-by-day travel plan
- **Budget estimation** — calculates total trip cost including accommodation and activities

## Stack

Streamlit · OpenAI Agents SDK · Logfire tracing · Google Gemini / GPT-4o
