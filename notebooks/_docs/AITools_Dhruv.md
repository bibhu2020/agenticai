# CREATE Prompt Engineering Techniques

## C – Context (what you know)
Provide background information that helps the model understand the situation.

**Example (Context):**  
I am planning a 5-day trip to Goa in December with my family. We prefer beaches, light sightseeing, and good food. Our budget is moderate, and we will stay near Baga Beach.

---

## R – Request (what you want)
Clearly state what you are asking the model to do.

**Example (Request):**  
Create a detailed travel itinerary for our Goa trip.

---

## E – Example (show an example)
Give a sample of the kind of response or format you like.

**Example (Example):**
- Day 1: Arrival + nearby beach  
- Day 2: North Goa sightseeing  
- Day 3: Water activities  
- Day 4: South Goa  
- Day 5: Shopping + departure  

---

## A – Answer (what you expect)
Describe the quality, depth, or structure of the output.

**Example (Answer Expectation):**  
The itinerary should include daily activities, approximate timings, food recommendations, and travel tips.

---

## T – Task (what you want to do)
Define the final action or goal.

**Example (Task):**  
Plan a realistic, relaxed, and family-friendly Goa vacation schedule.

---

## E – Extract (what you want to extract)
Specify what information should be pulled out from the response.

**Example (Extract):**  
Extract the following from the itinerary:
- List of beaches to visit  
- Daily activity summary (1–2 lines per day)  
- Recommended restaurants  
- Estimated travel time between locations

# Final CREAT(E) Prompt – Travel Plan to Goa

## Context
I am planning a 5-day family trip to Goa in December. We prefer beaches, relaxed sightseeing, and local Goan food. Our budget is moderate, and we will stay near Baga Beach.

## Request
Create a detailed and realistic travel itinerary for our Goa trip.

## Example
A day-wise plan such as:
- Day 1: Arrival + nearby beach
- Day 2: North Goa sightseeing
- Day 3: Water activities
- Day 4: South Goa
- Day 5: Shopping + departure

## Answer Expectation
The itinerary should:
- Be family-friendly and not rushed  
- Include daily activities with approximate timings  
- Suggest good local restaurants or beach shacks  
- Provide helpful travel tips (transport, best time to visit places)

## Task
Plan a smooth, enjoyable, and well-balanced 5-day Goa vacation that mixes leisure, sightseeing, and food experiences.

## Extract
From the final itinerary, clearly extract and list:
- All beaches covered during the trip  
- A short daily summary (1–2 lines per day)  
- Recommended restaurants and food spots  
- Estimated travel time between major locations
